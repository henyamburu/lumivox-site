Comparing Two Budget Sleep Trackers for Your Lumivox Setup

The Fitbit Inspire 3 and Xiaomi Smart Band 10 are two of the most accessible entry-level sleep trackers available. Both track sleep stages, heart rate, and blood oxygen. Both connect to a companion app where you can view sleep trends. And both fit comfortably enough for overnight wear.

The differences come down to how each device presents data, how long the battery lasts between charges, and what setup is required to get the most out of the sleep tracking features.

Sleep Tracking: What Each Device Offers

Both devices estimate sleep stages — light, deep, and REM — using movement and heart rate data. These estimates are useful for observing whether your bedroom routine changes correspond with any shifts in sleep trend patterns over time. They are not medical-grade measurements, and both manufacturers state that their products are not designed for medical purposes.

Fitbit Inspire 3 (Google Health app)

  • Automatic sleep tracking — no manual activation required
  • Daily Sleep Score provided in the Google Health app
  • Sleep stages tracked: light, deep, REM
  • Smart Wake vibrating alarm — described by the manufacturer as designed to wake during an optimal sleep stage
  • Naps of at least 20 minutes are automatically detected, per Google Health support documentation
  • Skin temperature variation available in the app — not intended for medical purposes, per Google/Fitbit
  • Sleep Profile and advanced personalised coaching available with Google Health Premium subscription

Xiaomi Smart Band 10 (Mi Fitness app)

  • Sleep stage tracking included — light, deep, REM
  • Sleep score available; advanced monitoring must be enabled to obtain a detailed score
  • Sleep trend reports available on a daily, weekly, and monthly basis
  • 21-day sleep improvement plan available — must be enabled manually in the Mi Fitness app
  • Sleep Animal feature: the app generates a representative Sleep Animal based on the past 7 days of sleep data, per Xiaomi FAQ
  • Naps under 20 minutes are not recorded, per Xiaomi product page
  • All-day stress monitoring available — must be enabled manually
  • Not designed for medical purposes, per Xiaomi product page

Lumivox editorial note: The practical difference for most beginners is configuration. The Fitbit Inspire 3 presents sleep data in the Google Health app without requiring manual feature activation for core tracking. The Xiaomi Smart Band 10 offers a detailed sleep improvement plan, but both the plan and the detailed sleep score require manual enablement in Mi Fitness. Neither difference makes one device technically superior — they reflect different design choices about default behaviour.

Summary Guidance

If your priority is the most accessible app experience with minimal configuration required to start tracking sleep, the Fitbit Inspire 3 is worth considering. The Google Health app presents Sleep Score, sleep stages, and Smart Wake without requiring manual feature activation for core tracking.

If your priority is lower cost, longer battery life, or access to a built-in sleep improvement plan, the Xiaomi Smart Band 10 is worth considering. The 21-day sleep improvement plan and detailed sleep score require manual setup in Mi Fitness, but are included without a subscription.

For most beginner Lumivox users, the Fitbit Inspire 3 is a reasonable default because of the minimal configuration needed to get started. The Xiaomi Smart Band 10 is a strong alternative for users who prioritise battery life or want to avoid an optional subscription tier.

Important: Neither device is a medical tool. Both manufacturers explicitly state that their products are not intended to predict, diagnose, prevent, or cure any disease. QuestionAnswer
Do I need Google Health Premium? No. Core sleep tracking — including sleep stages, Sleep Score, and Smart Wake — is available without a subscription. Google Health Premium (which replaces Fitbit Premium) is required for some advanced features, including Sleep Profile and personalised coaching. Check the current Google Health app for the latest feature tiers, as these may change.
Is Xiaomi Smart Band 10 suitable for sleep trend tracking? According to Xiaomi, it includes sleep stage monitoring, a sleep score, and a 21-day sleep improvement plan. Xiaomi states that the product and its features are not designed for medical purposes and are not intended to predict, diagnose, prevent, or cure any disease.
Can either device control smart bulbs or plugs? No. Both devices are sleep tracking tools in the Lumivox setup. They do not control or integrate with smart bulbs, smart plugs, or bedroom automation hardware.
What is the minimum nap length that gets recorded? Both devices have a 20-minute minimum threshold. The Google Health support documentation states that Fitbit devices detect naps of at least 20 minutes. The Xiaomi Smart Band 10 product page states that naps under 20 minutes will not be recorded.
Should I buy Smart Band 9 or Smart Band 10? The Smart Band 10 is the current model. It includes an updated display (1.72" vs. 1.62"), improved brightness (1500 nits vs. 1200 nits), a faster charger (approx. 1 hour vs. longer on Band 9), and an enhanced sleep management feature set. The Smart Band 9 remains a comparable option if the Band 10 is unavailable in your region or at a higher relative price.
Is the Fitbit app still called Fitbit? As of May 19, 2026, the Fitbit app has been rebranded as the Google Health app, per Google's official support documentation. All Fitbit device features are now accessed through the Google Health app.

About

About Lumivox

Lumivox is focused on building simple systems that connect wearable data with your home environment.

The Goal

Turn data into real-world results

Most people have a fitness tracker on their wrist and a smart bulb in their lamp — but the two never talk to each other. Lumivox bridges that gap.

We build practical systems that connect what your body tells you with what your home does about it. Better sleep. Better energy. Better daily performance.

No clutter. No complexity. Just systems that work.

What we focus on

Simple systems, not complicated tech setups

Affordable devices that deliver real results

Connecting wearable data to your home environment

Consistency over complexity — habits that last

Built for real daily use, not tech enthusiasts only

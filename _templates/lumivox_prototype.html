Oura Ring Gen 4 — the most accurate sleep tracker we've tested

By Lumivox Editors · Updated May 2025 9.1 / 10 Setup: Beginner-friendly

Most sleep trackers make you choose: accuracy or comfort. The Oura Ring Gen 4 largely sidesteps that trade-off. It sits on your finger, not your wrist, which means no screen to ignore and no charging interruption at 2am. After six weeks of parallel testing against a Whoop 4.0 and Apple Watch Ultra 2, the Oura consistently produced the most granular and actionable sleep data of the three.

The Gen 4 upgrade brings a new sensor array — infrared LEDs and a photodetector positioned to capture pulse from multiple points on the finger. The practical result is improved heart-rate accuracy during restless sleep, which is exactly when competing wrist-based trackers tend to lose signal quality.

What it actually tells you each morning

The Oura app surfaces three scores: Sleep, Readiness, and Activity. Sleep breaks down into Total Sleep, REM, Deep, and Light stages with a timeline. Readiness aggregates HRV, resting heart rate, body temperature deviation, and previous night's sleep into a single number that actually correlates, in our testing, with how alert you feel. Skin temperature sensing — flagging deviations as small as 0.1°C — is genuinely useful for catching illness before symptoms arrive.

The membership question

The ring costs $349–$549 depending on finish, plus a $5.99/month membership for full data access. You get six months free. After that, without a membership, the app shows only a limited summary. This is the most legitimate criticism of the product — though for anyone serious about sleep tracking, the membership cost is comparable to a single GP appointment.

Pros

  • Most comfortable 24/7 wearable we've tested
  • Excellent HRV and temperature accuracy
  • 7-day battery (real-world average)
  • No screen — no light disruption at night
  • Women's health cycle tracking built in

Cons

  • $5.99/month after 6-month free trial
  • No GPS or display
  • Ring sizing needs a 2-week kit trial
  • Not ideal if you dislike wearing rings

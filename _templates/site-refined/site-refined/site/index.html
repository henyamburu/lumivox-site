The problem

The problem is not more sleep data. It is knowing what to do with it.

Most trackers can show a pattern. Lumivox focuses on the next practical bedroom change.

You see the data

Sleep scores, wake times, and trend lines can show what happened overnight.

The room stays the same

The lights, plug-in devices, and wind-down timing often keep running on old habits.

You need a simple next step

Lumivox gives you a small routine to test before you change too many things at once.
